Privacy policy

Applies to the Gharcha app for Android and iOS. Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Gharcha does not collect, upload or share your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

  • Expenses, categories, groups, budgets, income, reminders, shop tabs, home services, gold and chit entries.
  • Household members' names, avatars, optional UPI IDs and optional PINs. PINs are stored as salted hashes, not as the digits you type.
  • App preferences, points, streaks and badges.

All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it (CSV, PDF, a share card, a reminder message).

Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you tap the mic to add an expense by voice. Speech is converted to text by your phone's own speech service; Gharcha does not record or keep audio.
  • Notifications: used for the reminders, bill alerts and budget alerts you set up. They are scheduled on the phone.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you attach a receipt picture to an expense. The picture is kept in the app's private storage.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: the check is done by your phone; Gharcha only learns whether it passed and never sees biometric data.
  • Notification access (Android, optional): only if you turn on Payment capture. Gharcha then reads the alerts of your SMS app as they arrive, keeps only those that contain a money amount, and turns completed payments into suggestions you can add or dismiss. The text is processed and stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can switch it off at any time in the phone's notification access settings.
  • Clipboard: read only at the moment you tap the paste button to fill in a payment message.

Payments

"Pay by UPI" opens your own UPI app with the amount filled in. Gharcha never sees or handles the payment, your bank details or the result.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Me → Your data → Erase all removes everything in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on the project's GitHub page, linked from the home page.
